What happens when it can't help

You stay in control.

Your AI receptionist captures the caller's details and the reason for their call. If a caller needs human assistance or has an urgent request, the receptionist flags the enquiry and sends you a notification so you can follow up. It does not make promises on your behalf or invent answers when it does not know something.

Why it matters

A caller who reaches voicemail rarely leaves one. They dial the next number.

Most home-service businesses miss a large share of their calls during busy stretches: everyone's on a job, or it's after hours. Each of those is a customer ready to book, handed to whoever picks up. An AI receptionist means there's always someone answering, even when there isn't.

Questions

Before you get started.

Does Synovatics replace my existing business phone number?

No. You keep your existing business number. Call forwarding is configured to send unanswered or after-hours calls to your Synovatics AI receptionist.

Do I need to buy new equipment?

No. You do not need special hardware or a new phone system.

Can I choose when the AI answers calls?

Yes. You can configure it to answer after-hours calls, missed calls, or overflow calls when your team is busy.

Can the AI book appointments?

Currently, the receptionist captures appointment requests and sends the details to you. Confirmed bookings and calendar integrations depend on the services configured for your business.

What happens if the AI cannot answer a question?

It avoids guessing, captures the enquiry, and sends it to you for follow-up.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. All plans are month to month. You can cancel according to the cancellation policy.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. You pay monthly, with no long-term commitment.
