How it works

It picks up when you can't.

No new phone system, no app to learn, nothing to install. It sits behind your existing line and catches what would otherwise go to voicemail.

Step 1

It answers

A caller reaches your line while you're busy or closed. The receptionist picks up in your company's name. Warm, calm, professional.

Step 2

It sorts and captures

It works out whether the call is urgent or routine, then takes the caller's name, number, location, and what they need.

Step 3

It hands you the lead

The details reach you right away, so you can call back and book the job instead of losing it to the next name on the list.

Why it matters

A caller who reaches voicemail rarely leaves one. They dial the next number.

Most service businesses miss a large share of their calls during busy stretches: everyone's on a job, or it's after hours. Each of those is a customer ready to book, handed to whoever picks up. An automated receptionist means there's always someone answering, even when there isn't.
