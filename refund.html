Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This is a general template. It isn't a substitute for legal advice.

1. Trial

New customers can start a 7-day trial with no card required. The trial covers after-hours and overflow calls only, with no change to your existing phone setup. At the end of the 7 days, you decide whether to continue to a paid subscription.

2. No Contract

Subscriptions are month-to-month. There's no long-term contract and no setup fee, and you can cancel at any time.

3. How to Cancel

Email marie@synovatics.com with your business name to cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, you'll keep access to the Service through the period you've already paid for.

4. Refunds

Because each month's fee covers access to a receptionist already built and running for your business, payments already made are generally non-refundable. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us within 7 days of the charge and we'll look into it, a partial refund may be issued at our discretion.

5. Overage Charges

Minutes used beyond your plan's included amount are billed per minute at the rate for your plan, added to that month's charge.

6. Changing Plans

Email us to move to a different plan. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to marie@synovatics.com.
