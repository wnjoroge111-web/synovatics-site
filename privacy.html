Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This is a general template describing how Synovatics handles information. It isn't a substitute for legal advice.

1. Overview

This policy explains what information Synovatics collects when you use our website or our automated receptionist service (the "Service"), and how it's used.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact information you give us — your name, business name, email address, and phone number, for example when you request a demo or start a trial.
  • Call data — when the Service handles a call for your business, it captures the caller's name, phone number, and the details of their request. Calls may be recorded and transcribed to do this, and to help us provide, support, and improve the Service.
  • Payment information — subscription payments are handled directly by our payment processor. We do not store your card details ourselves.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information to deliver the Service (including routing captured leads to you), to build and tune your tailored receptionist, to bill your subscription, and to respond to you directly.

4. Third-Party Providers

The Service relies on a small number of third-party providers to operate, including a voice AI platform to run the receptionist, an automation platform to route leads, email to deliver them, and a payment processor to handle billing. Each only processes the information needed to perform its part of the Service.

5. Data Retention

We retain call and lead data for as long as your account is active. After your subscription ends, we delete this data within 30 days, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or we're required to keep it longer for legal reasons.

6. Your Choices

You can email marie@synovatics.com at any time to request a copy of the information we hold about you or your business, or to ask us to delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children

The Service is intended for businesses, not for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted here with an updated date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to marie@synovatics.com.
